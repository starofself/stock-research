흡수하는 물리적 기반이 된다.</span></p><p class="se-text-paragraph se-text-paragraph-align-" id="SE-062b6b18-378d-4e76-b250-5a7c1bc83906" style="line-height:2.0;"><span class="se-fs-fs19 se-ff-nanumbareunhipi
