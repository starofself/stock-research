획기적으로 향상시킨다.</span></p></li></ol><p class="se-text-paragraph se-text-paragraph-align-" id="SE-d5a56727-d388-45ef-90a5-b09872292692" style="line-height:2.0;"><span class="se-fs-fs19 se-ff-nanumbareunhipi se-weight-unset se-style-unset" id="SE-51ec6666-1791-4772-b8d3-a4073a8c3ef2" style="color:#000000;">2.3 이더넷(RoCE v2)의 도전과 아이크래프트의 방어 전략</span></p><p class="se-text-paragraph se-text-paragraph-align-" id="SE-76ad365b-35b7-45a9-b473-183514092354" style="line-height:2.0;"><span class="se-fs-fs15 se-ff-nanumbareunhipi se-weight-unset se-style-unset"
